Electrical trouble is not always caused by the part that stopped working. A dead outlet is often fed by a tripped GFCI in another room. A breaker trips because something on that circuit is wrong. Flickering lights usually mean a loose connection, not a bad fixture.

Our goal is to locate the real problem, make the repair safely, and confirm that the circuit is working properly.

Professional troubleshooting

The right repair starts with careful troubleshooting.

Here is what we check:

  • Breakers and fuses
  • GFCI outlets
  • Switches and receptacles
  • Voltage at outlets and equipment
  • Wiring connections
  • Loose or damaged wires
  • Grounding
  • Electrical panels and subpanels
  • Appliance and equipment circuits
  • Signs of overheating or damage

In one home, several receptacles had stopped working. The electrician found a loose neutral connection in an outlet along with unsafe wiring at the dishwasher. When the trouble runs past a single repair, we talk about rewiring the affected area. After correcting both issues, the circuit returned to normal operation.

At another home, a breaker that would not reset was traced to an outdoor-lighting circuit. Isolating the lights restored the rest of the circuit, and further testing indicated a likely short in the underground wiring. Resetting the breaker or swapping the outlet leaves the actual fault sitting there.

Common service calls

Electrical problems we diagnose and repair in Roanoke.

Outlets That Do Not Work

A bad receptacle, a tripped GFCI, a loose wire, or a fault further down the circuit will all kill power. We test the circuit to find where power is being lost.

Breakers That Keep Tripping

A breaker that keeps tripping is reacting to something. An overload, a short, damaged wiring, or a bad appliance. Diagnose it rather than reset it again. If the equipment itself is worn out, we look at whether it needs panel work.

GFCI Problems

We replace failed GFCIs, restore power to protected outlets, and correct wiring that keeps a GFCI from operating properly in kitchens, bathrooms, garages, basements, and outdoor areas.

Flickering or Failed Lights

Lighting trouble starts at the bulb, the fixture, the switch, or the wiring. If a new bulb does not fix it, we look behind the switch and the fixture.

Switch Problems

We troubleshoot and replace standard switches, three-way switches, dimmers, combination switches, and controls for lights and exhaust fans. We also handle planned outlet, lighting, and control upgrades.

Loose, Damaged, or Unsafe Wiring

Loose connections cause power that comes and goes, and they run hot. We repair damaged conductors, loose neutrals, poor connections, and improperly installed wiring.

Problems When an Appliance Runs

If the trouble starts when the dishwasher or the AC kicks on, we find out whether it is the outlet, the circuit, or the appliance.

Outdoor Electrical Problems

We troubleshoot exterior outlets, GFCIs, lighting, pool subpanels, and underground circuits exposed to moisture, weather, and ground movement.
