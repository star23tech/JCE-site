Professional troubleshooting

The right repair starts with careful troubleshooting.

Depending on the symptoms, our electricians may check:

  • Breakers and fuses
  • GFCI outlets
  • Switches and receptacles
  • Voltage at outlets and equipment
  • Wiring connections
  • Loose or damaged wires
  • Grounding
  • Electrical panels and subpanels
  • Appliance and equipment circuits
  • Signs of overheating or damage

In one home, several receptacles had stopped working. The electrician found a loose neutral connection in an outlet along with unsafe wiring at the dishwasher. When problems extend beyond a focused repair, we can also discuss rewiring the affected area. After correcting both issues, the circuit returned to normal operation.

At another home, a breaker that would not reset was traced to an outdoor-lighting circuit. Isolating the lights restored the rest of the circuit, and further testing indicated a likely short in the underground wiring. Finding the cause matters; simply resetting a breaker or replacing an outlet may leave the underlying fault in place.

Common service calls

Electrical problems we diagnose and repair in Roanoke.

Outlets That Do Not Work

A failed receptacle, tripped GFCI, loose wire, breaker issue, or fault elsewhere on the circuit can interrupt power. We test the circuit to find where power is being lost.

Breakers That Keep Tripping

A repeatedly tripping breaker may be responding to an overload, short, damaged wiring, appliance, or other equipment. It should be diagnosed instead of repeatedly reset. If testing points to deteriorated equipment, we can assess whether panel work is appropriate.

GFCI Problems

We replace failed GFCIs, restore power to protected outlets, and correct wiring that keeps a GFCI from operating properly in kitchens, bathrooms, garages, basements, and outdoor areas.

Flickering or Failed Lights

Lighting trouble can start at the bulb, fixture, switch, wiring, or circuit. If a new bulb does not solve it, we can inspect the electrical connections behind the switch and fixture.

Switch Problems

We troubleshoot and replace standard switches, three-way switches, dimmers, combination switches, and controls for lights and exhaust fans. We also handle planned outlet, lighting, and control upgrades.

Loose, Damaged, or Unsafe Wiring

Loose connections can cause intermittent power, overheating, and safety concerns. We repair damaged conductors, loose neutrals, poor connections, and improperly installed wiring.

Problems When an Appliance Runs

If trouble starts when a heater, dishwasher, air conditioner, or other appliance operates, we can determine whether the fault is in the outlet, circuit, breaker, wiring, or connected equipment.

Outdoor Electrical Problems

We troubleshoot exterior outlets, GFCIs, lighting, pool subpanels, and underground circuits exposed to moisture, weather, and ground movement.
