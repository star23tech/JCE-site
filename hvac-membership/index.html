A practical system review

What We Check During HVAC Maintenance.

The exact maintenance steps depend on your equipment and operating conditions. A typical visit may include checks of the following areas:

  • Thermostat operation
  • Air filter condition
  • Electrical connections
  • Capacitors and electrical components
  • Indoor and outdoor coils
  • Condensate drain and drainage
  • Blower operation
  • Airflow
  • Temperature performance
  • Refrigerant operating conditions where appropriate
  • Safety controls
  • Visible equipment condition
  • Unusual noise or vibration
  • General system operation

If we find something that needs attention, we'll explain what we found and discuss the available options before additional repair work is performed.

Straightforward recommendations

Maintenance Without the Hard Sell.

Our goal during a maintenance visit is to inspect the equipment, perform the appropriate maintenance checks, and let you know what we find. If the system is operating properly, we'll tell you that. If something needs attention, we'll explain the issue and your options before any additional work is performed.

Membership is intended to help maintain the system and identify developing issues. It does not mean that every part or future repair is automatically covered.

If service is needed, learn more about AC repair, heating repair, and heat pump services. You can also review JCE's transparent pricing information before you schedule.

Common membership questions

HVAC Membership FAQ.

Is the membership price per HVAC system?

Membership pricing shown is for a standard residential HVAC system. Multi-system pricing can be discussed with JCE.

When are the two seasonal visits performed?

The cooling visit is generally scheduled before or during cooling season, and the heating visit before or during heating season.

Are repairs included in the membership?

No. Maintenance and membership benefits are included according to the selected plan. Repair work and replacement parts are separate unless specifically stated.

What happens if you find a problem?

JCE will explain the issue and recommended options before additional repair work is performed.

Can I join if JCE did not install my HVAC system?

In most cases, yes. Contact JCE with information about your existing system and we can confirm eligibility.

How do I sign up?

Call JCE at (540) 300-1982 or use the online contact form to ask about the plan that fits your home and system.
