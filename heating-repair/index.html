We find the cause of the heating problem.

Heating troubleshooting

Common heating problems we diagnose and repair.

Heat Is Not Coming On

If you turn the thermostat up and nothing happens, the break is somewhere between the thermostat and the blower. We find where.

We follow the heating sequence until we find where it stops.

Heat Pump Is Running but the House Is Still Cold

Low refrigerant, weak airflow, or a bad reversing valve all cut how much heat you get.

Our technicians have worked on heat pumps with low refrigerant, control problems, reversing-valve concerns, and systems that were not operating correctly during heating mode.

We check how the complete system is operating instead of judging it only by whether the outdoor unit is running.

Blower Fan Is Not Working

Our work records include blower motors that hummed but would not start, motors with failed windings, loose electrical connections, and motors that needed replacement.

We check the motor, capacitor, wiring, and power going to the blower to determine why it is not working.

Heat Pump Keeps Shutting Down or Locking Out

Heat pumps use controls to manage heating, cooling, and defrost operation. Our technicians have diagnosed systems that entered lockout because they were not operating correctly during the heating and defrost cycle.

A system that keeps resetting, stopping, or displaying an error should be checked before the problem gets worse.

Auxiliary or Emergency Heat Is Running Too Much

Heat pumps fall back on electric auxiliary heat when they need help. If auxiliary heat runs too much, the house stays warm and your power bill goes through the roof.

The cause is usually the heat pump, the thermostat, or how the system was set up.

We work on heat pumps, electric backup heat, and dual-fuel systems, and we check whether yours is running the way it should.

Furnace Is Running Too Hot

A furnace that runs too hot shuts itself down. Our service records include a gas furnace that was running hot because airflow was restricted by a dirty screen before the heat exchanger.

The technician cleaned and serviced the furnace and also checked the high-limit control, return airflow, and thermostat setup. Airflow is an important part of safe furnace operation, so overheating should not be ignored.

Thermostat or Control Problems

Sometimes the heating equipment is capable of working, but the controls are keeping it from operating correctly.

Our team has serviced and replaced thermostats, corrected thermostat wiring and programming, reset controls, and worked with thermostats controlling heat pumps, gas furnaces, electric heat, and dual-fuel systems.

A thermostat problem looks exactly like a dead furnace. We check it either way.
