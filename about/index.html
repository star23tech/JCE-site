Owner John W. Calhoun leads a team with more than 20 years of field experience across electrical work. The company supports projects ranging from residential repairs and older-home rewiring to commercial construction, custom remodels, multifamily work, and residential and light commercial HVAC service.

Every property and project presents different needs. The team begins by listening, assessing the situation, and working with the customer toward a practical solution.
