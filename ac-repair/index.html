Professional diagnostics

We diagnose the problem before recommending a repair.

An AC system can stop cooling for many different reasons. That is why proper testing matters. Depending on the problem, our technicians may check:

  • Refrigerant pressures
  • Supply and return air temperatures
  • Capacitors and contactors
  • Voltage and electrical connections
  • Blower and fan motors
  • Evaporator and outdoor coils
  • Air filters
  • Condensate drains and float switches
  • Thermostats and control boards
  • System error codes

We have seen systems shut down because a clogged drain tripped a safety switch, compressors that would not start because of failed capacitors, and blown fuses caused by damaged sensor wires rubbing against metal cabinets. Replacing one failed part does not help if the problem that caused it is still there.

Common service calls

Common AC problems we repair in Roanoke.

AC Not Cooling or Blowing Warm Air

Possible causes include low refrigerant, dirty coils, airflow restrictions, failed capacitors, faulty TXVs, and compressor problems. We check how the system is operating before recommending the next step.

AC Will Not Turn On

We test capacitors, contactors, fuses, float switches, wiring, and controls to learn why the system stopped before replacing parts.

Water Leaking From Your AC

We can clear clogged drains, inspect the pan and drain line, and verify that water flows properly again.

Low Refrigerant or Refrigerant Leaks

We check system pressures and signs of leaks to determine whether repair or further leak testing makes sense.

Frozen AC Coils

We inspect airflow, coils, refrigerant levels, and refrigerant-control components to identify why the coil froze.

Blower, Fan, and Motor Problems

We diagnose motors by checking voltage, capacitors, wiring, and motor windings before replacing failed parts.

Electrical, Capacitor, and Control Problems

We test capacitors, contactors, fuses, wiring, thermostats, safety switches, and control boards so the repair reflects what the system is actually doing.
