What to expect

Our EV charger installation process.

Review the Charger Requirements

We look at the charging equipment's electrical rating, connection type, and installation requirements. Already bought a charger? Send us the specs before we start.

Check the Electrical Panel

We check your open breaker space and whether the panel will carry the charger.

Plan the Circuit and Route

We determine the appropriate breaker, wiring, and circuit based on the charger and the distance between the panel and charging location.

Install the Electrical Connection

We run wiring from the panel, install the breaker, and provide the appropriate hardwired connection or receptacle for the charging equipment.

Test the Installation

We test the circuit and connections to confirm that the new charging circuit is operating correctly before the electrical work is finished.

Every installation is different

Plan around the panel, parking space, and charger.

Panel Capacity

A charger does not automatically require a new panel. We review the existing equipment, breaker space, and electrical load before recommending any panel upgrade.

Wiring Distance

A charger near the panel is a short, simple run. One across the garage or on the far side of the house takes more wiring work and more planning.

Indoor or Outdoor Location

We install them in garages and outdoors. Exterior charging equipment and electrical components must be appropriate for weather exposure and the proposed location.

EV charger FAQs

Answers to common home-charging questions.

What type of EV charger should I install?

Many homeowners choose a Level 2 charger for everyday home charging. The right equipment depends on your vehicle, desired charging speed, and home electrical system.

Does an EV charger need a dedicated circuit?

Many home EV chargers do. A dedicated circuit uses a breaker and wiring designed for the charger's load rather than sharing power with other outlets or equipment.

Can my current panel handle an EV charger?

It depends on the available breaker space, existing electrical demand, service capacity, and the requirements of the selected charger. We review these factors before planning the circuit.

Will I need to replace my electrical panel?

Not always. If you have the capacity and an open slot, you do not need a new panel. If it cannot support the load, we explain the available upgrade options.

How large a circuit does an EV charger need?

That depends on the equipment. Chargers have different amperage and wiring requirements, so we size the circuit from the specifications of the unit being installed.

Can the charger be installed in my garage?

In many homes, yes. We consider the panel location, parking position, proposed charger location, and the route needed for the new wiring.

Can an EV charger be installed outdoors?

Yes, as long as the charger is rated for outdoor use and the boxes, wiring, and protection match the location.

Can I plan the installation before buying an EV?

Yes, and it is the smart order to do it in. Tell us the vehicle or the charger you expect and we will price the electrical ahead of time, or fold it into other residential electrical upgrades.
