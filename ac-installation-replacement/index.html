Repair or replacement?

When does an AC system need to be replaced?

Not every broken air conditioner needs replacing. Plenty of calls end with a new capacitor, a motor, or a drain repair, and a straight AC repair is the cheaper answer. Replacement makes sense when the compressor is gone, the coil leaks, or the unit still runs on R-22. At that point you are putting good money into old equipment.

We have also seen repairs priced so close to a new system that replacing it was the better deal. We look at the whole system before we tell you that. Businesses and property managers can also see our commercial HVAC services.

Helpful answers

AC installation and replacement FAQs.

How do I know if I should repair or replace my AC?

It depends on what failed, the age and condition of the equipment, and repair cost. A small electrical failure is a repair. A dead compressor, a leaking coil, or repeat refrigerant loss is where replacement wins.

Can you replace both indoor and outdoor equipment?

Yes. Some replacements involve both portions of the system. We determine what needs replacement based on the condition and design of the existing equipment.

What if my AC still works but does not cool well?

Poor cooling does not automatically mean replacement. Dirty coils, clogged filters, low refrigerant, and bad capacitors are all repairs. Diagnosis should come first.

Should I replace an older R-22 air conditioner?

Old R-22 equipment still runs. It just gets harder to justify putting money into. We inspect the equipment and explain the practical options.

What happens if my compressor fails?

Sometimes compressor replacement makes sense. In other cases, equipment condition or repair cost makes full replacement the better choice. We assess the complete system.

Can you replace a leaking evaporator coil?

Yes. Whether replacing the coil or the entire system makes more sense depends on the condition of the remaining equipment.

Do you install heat pumps too?

Yes. We install and replace heat pump systems for Roanoke homes. Visit our Heat Pump Services page for more information.

How long should I wait if my AC is starting to fail?

If it needs frequent service, discuss replacement before it stops completely. That gives you time to understand your options instead of making a rushed summer decision.
