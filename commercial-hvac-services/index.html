Repair and troubleshooting

We test the system before deciding what needs to be repaired.

Commercial gear fails for the same reasons a home system does. It just runs harder and sits somewhere less convenient. We work on rooftop units, package systems, split systems, exhaust fans, and heat pumps.

  • Failed capacitors
  • Blower and fan motors
  • Contactors and relays
  • Control boards
  • Thermostats
  • Refrigerant leaks
  • Low refrigerant
  • TXV problems
  • Dirty coils
  • Clogged drains
  • Float switches
  • Compressor problems
  • Electrical faults
  • Airflow restrictions

We work it the same way we work a residential AC repair. Test it, find the cause, fix that, then prove it runs.

Field-tested diagnostics

Commercial HVAC problems we have handled.

Older Package Unit

A package unit was barely cooling and the thermostat cut in and out. It was old R-22 equipment running hard, and the compressor was on its way out.

Multiple Gym Systems

One unit had a burned contactor coil. Another had a defrost board getting power but not passing the right voltage through.

Rooftop Exhaust Equipment

An exhaust fan had good control voltage but a dead motor. We replaced it and ran the fan before we left.
