Repair and troubleshooting

We test the system before deciding what needs to be repaired.

Commercial equipment can fail for many of the same reasons as residential systems, but the equipment and operating conditions can be more complex. Our service history includes rooftop units, package systems, split systems, ductless equipment, exhaust fans, and heat pumps.

  • Failed capacitors
  • Blower and fan motors
  • Contactors and relays
  • Control boards
  • Thermostats
  • Refrigerant leaks
  • Low refrigerant
  • TXV problems
  • Dirty coils
  • Clogged drains
  • Float switches
  • Compressor problems
  • Electrical faults
  • Airflow restrictions

Our diagnostic approach follows the same principle used in residential AC repair: test the equipment, find the cause, make the right repair, and verify normal operation.

Field-tested diagnostics

Commercial HVAC problems we have handled.

Older Package Unit

A package unit was not cooling well and had an intermittent thermostat issue. Testing showed that the older R-22 equipment was operating inefficiently and could be approaching compressor failure.

Multiple Gym Systems

One unit had a failed contactor coil. Another had a bad defrost control board receiving power but not sending the correct voltage to the contactor.

Rooftop Exhaust Equipment

An exhaust fan was diagnosed with a failed motor after proper control voltage was confirmed. The motor was replaced and the fan tested for normal operation.
