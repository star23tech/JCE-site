Electrical repair FAQs.

Should I keep resetting a tripping breaker?

No. If a breaker trips again after the immediate load is removed, stop resetting it and arrange an evaluation.

Do you repair both homes and businesses?

Yes. John Calhoun Electric provides residential and commercial electrical service throughout its Roanoke-area coverage.

Can you update old wiring during a repair?

The team can evaluate old wiring and discuss the appropriate repair or modernization scope for the property.
