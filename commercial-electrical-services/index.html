Commercial repair and troubleshooting

We test the system to find the real cause.

A dead light or outlet points somewhere. A breaker, a relay, a loose connection, or the wiring behind it. We test before we replace.

  • Breakers and panels
  • Voltage
  • Wiring connections
  • Receptacles and switches
  • Lighting circuits
  • Motors and controls
  • Relays
  • Dedicated equipment circuits
  • Service equipment
  • Signs of overheating or damage

One commercial job traced back to a failed heater fan motor. Another lost a leg of power, and we found heavy corrosion around the meter and service gear. That kind of incoming-power trouble usually means a service equipment replacement.

Finding the cause is the whole job. Parts are the easy part.
