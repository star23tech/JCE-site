Commercial repair and troubleshooting

We test the system to find the real cause.

A failed light, outlet, or piece of equipment can point to a problem with a fixture, breaker, relay, motor, control, connection, wiring, or service component. We troubleshoot before deciding what needs repair.

  • Breakers and panels
  • Voltage
  • Wiring connections
  • Receptacles and switches
  • Lighting circuits
  • Motors and controls
  • Relays
  • Dedicated equipment circuits
  • Service equipment
  • Signs of overheating or damage

At one commercial property, testing traced an electrical problem to a failed heater fan motor. At another, the loss of one leg of power led our electrician to heavy corrosion and deteriorated equipment around the meter and service area. Similar incoming-power problems may require a broader service-equipment replacement.

The important step is finding the cause instead of replacing parts until something works.

New Circuits and Receptacles
