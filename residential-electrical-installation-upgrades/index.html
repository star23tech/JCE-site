Reliable power for new equipment

New circuits and dedicated power.

Some equipment needs its own circuit. Sharing one with the rest of the room is how breakers start tripping.

On one job we ran a new circuit for a dehumidifier and sump pump. New wire, a breaker, a box, and GFCI protection.

If the panel has no room left or cannot carry the load, we will tell you. Sometimes there is another way around it. Sometimes it needs a panel upgrade.

A new circuit may include New cable from the panel The correct circuit breaker A new box and receptacle GFCI protection where required Testing after installation
