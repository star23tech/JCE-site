Reliable power for new equipment

New circuits and dedicated power.

Some appliances and equipment should have a dedicated circuit rather than share power with other devices. This can provide reliable operation without adding too much demand to an existing circuit.

In one residential project, our team installed a new circuit for a dehumidifier and sump pump using new wiring, a breaker, an electrical box, and GFCI protection.

If the existing panel does not have enough room or capacity for the planned load, we can explain whether a different approach or panel upgrade may be needed.
