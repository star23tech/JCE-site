Before your HVAC repair visit.

Check the basics

Confirm the thermostat setting and note whether the system or any breaker has shut off. Do not repeatedly reset a breaker.

Describe the symptoms

Share sounds, odors, timing, rooms affected, and whether the problem is constant or intermittent.

Keep access clear

Provide safe access to indoor equipment, outdoor equipment, thermostats, panels, and relevant controls.
