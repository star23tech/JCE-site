Outlets & Switches

Replace Standard Outlet

Typical price$150–$225 for the first; about $50 each additional

What might make the price higher

Damaged box; no ground; aluminum wiring; USB or tamper-resistant device

What might make the price lower

Several outlets completed during the same visit

Add New Outlet

Typical price$250–$500

What might make the price higher

Finished walls that require fishing wire; plaster or brick; long distance from an existing power source; new circuit required

What might make the price lower

Open attic, basement or crawlspace access; nearby circuit

Replace / Install GFCI Outlet

Typical price$150–$275

What might make the price higher

Outdoor weatherproof/in-use cover; troubleshooting a tripping problem; no equipment ground

What might make the price lower

One GFCI protecting several downstream outlets

Replace Light Switch

Typical price$110–$200 standard; $150–$275 dimmer or smart

What might make the price higher

3-way or 4-way switching; smart switch requiring a neutral that is not currently present

What might make the price lower

Several switches replaced during the same visit

Lighting & Fans

Replace Light Fixture

Typical price$150–$350 standard; $350–$800+ chandelier or high ceiling

What might make the price higher

Ceiling over 10 feet; heavy fixture requiring a brace; no existing electrical box; extensive fixture assembly

What might make the price lower

Like-for-like replacement using an existing electrical box

Replace / Install Ceiling Fan

Typical price$200–$400 replacement; $450–$850 new location

What might make the price higher

Fan-rated brace required; high or vaulted ceiling; new switch or wall control

What might make the price lower

Existing fan-rated box and wiring

Smoke / CO Detector Replacement

Typical price$75–$150 per device

What might make the price higher

Missing hardwired interconnect; high ceilings

What might make the price lower

Several devices replaced during the same visit

Circuits & Breakers

Replace Circuit Breaker

Typical price$175–$350 standard; $250–$450 AFCI/GFCI

What might make the price higher

Obsolete panel such as Federal Pacific or Zinsco; hard-to-find parts; damaged bus

What might make the price lower

Common panel brand and breaker readily available

Install New Dedicated Circuit

Typical price$350–$800

What might make the price higher

Long wire run; finished walls; full panel; permit requirements

What might make the price lower

Short run from panel; open framing

Install 240V Circuit

Typical price$500–$1,500

What might make the price higher

Higher amperage requirements; long wire run; limited panel capacity; permit requirements

What might make the price lower

Short run; open access; available panel capacity
