Electrical Pricing

Typical pricing for common residential electrical work. See our full Electrical Services.

Service & Troubleshooting

  • Electrical Diagnostic / Service Call

    Typical price$99

    Price may be higher when: After-hours or emergency service; intermittent problems that require additional tracing; long travel

    Price may be lower when: Fee is credited toward an approved repair

Outlets & Switches

  • Replace Standard Outlet

    Typical price$150–$225 for the first; about $50 each additional

    Price may be higher when: Damaged box; no ground; aluminum wiring; USB or tamper-resistant device

    Price may be lower when: Several outlets completed during the same visit

  • Add New Outlet

    Typical price$250–$500

    Price may be higher when: Finished walls that require fishing wire; plaster or brick; long distance from an existing power source; new circuit required

    Price may be lower when: Open attic, basement or crawlspace access; nearby circuit

  • Replace / Install GFCI Outlet

    Typical price$150–$275

    Price may be higher when: Outdoor weatherproof/in-use cover; troubleshooting a tripping problem; no equipment ground

    Price may be lower when: One GFCI protecting several downstream outlets

  • Replace Light Switch

    Typical price$110–$200 standard; $150–$275 dimmer or smart

    Price may be higher when: 3-way or 4-way switching; smart switch requiring a neutral that is not currently present

    Price may be lower when: Several switches replaced during the same visit

Lighting & Fans

  • Replace Light Fixture

    Typical price$150–$350 standard; $350–$800+ chandelier or high ceiling

    Price may be higher when: Ceiling over 10 feet; heavy fixture requiring a brace; no existing electrical box; extensive fixture assembly

    Price may be lower when: Like-for-like replacement using an existing electrical box

  • Replace / Install Ceiling Fan

    Typical price$200–$400 replacement; $450–$850 new location

    Price may be higher when: Fan-rated brace required; high or vaulted ceiling; new switch or wall control

    Price may be lower when: Existing fan-rated box and wiring

  • Smoke / CO Detector Replacement

    Typical price$75–$150 per device

    Price may be higher when: Missing hardwired interconnect; high ceilings

    Price may be lower when: Several devices replaced during the same visit

Circuits & Breakers

  • Replace Circuit Breaker

    Typical price$175–$350 standard; $250–$450 AFCI/GFCI

    Price may be higher when: Obsolete panel such as Federal Pacific or Zinsco; hard-to-find parts; damaged bus

    Price may be lower when: Common panel brand and breaker readily available

  • Install New Dedicated Circuit

    Typical price$350–$800

    Price may be higher when: Long wire run; finished walls; full panel; permit requirements

    Price may be lower when: Short run from panel; open framing

  • Install 240V Circuit

    Typical price$500–$1,500

    Price may be higher when: Higher amperage requirements; long wire run; limited panel capacity; permit requirements

    Price may be lower when: Short run; open access; available panel capacity

Panels, EV & Generator

  • Panel Upgrade / Replacement (200A)

    Typical price$2,500–$5,000+

    Price may be higher when: Meter base or service wire replacement; grounding upgrades; utility coordination; relocating the panel

    Price may be lower when: Replacement in the same location; existing service wiring in good condition

    Panel replacement details →

  • EV Charger Installation – Level 2

    Typical price$999–$2,500

    Price may be higher when: Long run to the garage; electrical panel upgrade required; hardwired 60A charger

    Price may be lower when: Panel located in or near garage; available breaker capacity

    EV charger installation details →

  • Whole-Home Surge Protector

    Typical price$350–$700

    Price may be higher when: Full panel or panel rework required

    Price may be lower when: Available panel space; installation performed along with other panel work

  • Generator Interlock + Inlet

    Typical price$1,250–$2,500

    Price may be higher when: Long run to generator inlet; panel not compatible with an interlock

    Price may be lower when: Inlet close to panel; compatible electrical panel

HVAC Pricing

Diagnostics & Maintenance

  • HVAC Diagnostic / Service Call

    Typical price$99

    Price may be lower when: The diagnostic fee is included in the approved repair price rather than added on top

    Typical price$75

    Good to know: Visual system check. This is different from the full hands-on tune-up.

  • AC Maintenance / Full Tune-Up

    Typical priceStarting at $175

    Good to know: Hands-on service including cleaning coils, checking filters, inspecting electrical components, and connecting gauges/checking system operation as appropriate.

    Price may be higher when: Multiple systems; heavily dirty coils; refrigerant required, which is priced separately

    Price may be lower when: Routine spring scheduling

  • Heating / Heat Pump Maintenance

    Typical priceStarting at $175

    Price may be higher when: Multiple systems; equipment located in attic or crawlspace

    Price may be lower when: Routine fall scheduling

Common Repairs

  • Capacitor Replacement

    Typical price$299–$350

    Price may be higher when: After-hours service; OEM-specific component; hard-start kit added

    Price may be lower when: Standard replacement part in stock

  • Contactor Replacement

    Typical price$299–$350

    Price may be higher when: After-hours service; related wiring damage

    Price may be lower when: Standard replacement part in stock

  • Thermostat Replacement

    Typical price$150–$300 standard; $250–$500 smart

    Price may be higher when: No C-wire; multi-stage heat pump or dual-fuel wiring

    Price may be lower when: Customer-supplied compatible thermostat; straightforward existing wiring

  • Condensate Drain Cleaning

    Typical price$150–$250+

    Price may be higher when: Severe clog; PVC drain line requiring disassembly and treatment; attic equipment

    Price may be lower when: Accessible drain that clears with vacuuming or flushing

  • Condenser Coil Cleaning

    Typical price$150–$300

    Good to know: Routine coil cleaning may already be included in a full tune-up. This range applies primarily to standalone or more involved coil cleaning.

    Price may be higher when: Heavily fouled coil; bent fins requiring combing; large equipment

    Price may be lower when: Cleaning performed during a full tune-up

Motors & Controls

  • Condenser Fan Motor Replacement

    Typical price$450–$750

    Price may be higher when: Variable-speed or OEM-only motor; fan blade replacement; equipment out of warranty

    Price may be lower when: Universal replacement motor; covered replacement part

  • Indoor Blower Motor Replacement

    Typical price$450–$900 standard; $900–$2,000+ variable-speed

    Price may be higher when: Variable-speed motor or control module; attic or crawlspace access

    Price may be lower when: Standard motor; accessible air handler; replacement part under warranty
