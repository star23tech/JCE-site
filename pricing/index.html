How Our Pricing Works

Service & Troubleshooting

  • Electrical Diagnostic / Service Call

    Typical price$99

    +May cost more when
    • After-hours or emergency service

    • Long travel
    −May cost less when

Outlets & Switches

  • Replace Standard Outlet

    Typical price$150–$225 for the first outlet, plus about $50 for each additional outlet

    +May cost more when
    • Damaged box
    • No ground
    • Aluminum wiring
    • USB or tamper-resistant device
    −May cost less when
    • Several outlets completed during the same visit

  • Add New Outlet

    Typical price$250–$500

    +May cost more when
    • Finished walls that require fishing wire
    • Plaster or brick
    • Long distance from an existing power source
    • New circuit required
    −May cost less when
    • Open attic, basement or crawlspace access
    • Nearby circuit

  • Replace / Install GFCI Outlet

    Typical price$150–$275

    +May cost more when
    • Outdoor weatherproof/in-use cover
    • Troubleshooting a tripping problem
    • No equipment ground
    −May cost less when
    • One GFCI protecting several downstream outlets

  • Replace Light Switch

    Typical price$110–$200 for a standard switch or $150–$275 for a dimmer or smart switch

    +May cost more when
    • 3-way or 4-way switching
    • Smart switch requiring a neutral that is not currently present
    −May cost less when
    • Several switches replaced during the same visit

Lighting & Fans

  • Replace Light Fixture

    Typical price$150–$350 for a standard fixture or $350–$800+ for a chandelier or high ceiling

    +May cost more when
    • Ceiling over 10 feet
    • Heavy fixture requiring a brace
    • No existing electrical box
    • Extensive fixture assembly
    −May cost less when
    • Like-for-like replacement using an existing electrical box

  • Replace / Install Ceiling Fan

    Typical price$200–$400 for a replacement or $450–$850 for a new location

    +May cost more when
    • Fan-rated brace required
    • High or vaulted ceiling
    • New switch or wall control
    −May cost less when
    • Existing fan-rated box and wiring

  • Smoke / CO Detector Replacement

    Typical price$75–$150 per device

    +May cost more when
    • Missing hardwired interconnect
    • High ceilings
    −May cost less when
    • Several devices replaced during the same visit

Circuits & Breakers

  • Replace Circuit Breaker

    Typical price$175–$350 for a standard breaker or $250–$450 for an AFCI/GFCI breaker

    +May cost more when
    • Obsolete panel such as Federal Pacific or Zinsco
    • Hard-to-find parts
    • Damaged bus
    −May cost less when
    • Common panel brand and breaker readily available

  • Install New Dedicated Circuit

    Typical price$350–$800

    +May cost more when
    • Long wire run
    • Finished walls
    • Full panel
    • Permit requirements
    −May cost less when
    • Short run from panel
    • Open framing

  • Install 240V Circuit

    Typical price$500–$1,500

    +May cost more when
    • Higher amperage requirements
    • Long wire run
    • Limited panel capacity
    • Permit requirements
    −May cost less when
    • Short run
    • Open access
    • Available panel capacity

Panels, EV & Generator

    • Grounding upgrades
    • Utility coordination

    −May cost less when
    • Replacement in the same location
    • Existing service wiring in good condition

  • EV Charger Installation

    Typical price$999–$2,500

    +May cost more when
    • Long run to the garage
    • Electrical panel upgrade required
    • Hardwired 60A charger
    −May cost less when
    • Panel located in or near garage
    • Available breaker capacity
    EV charger installation details →

  • Whole-Home Surge Protector

    Typical price$350–$700

    +May cost more when
    • Full panel or panel rework required
    −May cost less when
    • Available panel space
    • Installation performed along with other panel work

  • Generator Interlock + Inlet

    Typical price$1,250–$2,500

    +May cost more when
    • Long run to generator inlet

    −May cost less when
    • Inlet close to panel

    +May cost more when

Common Repairs

  • Capacitor Replacement

    Typical price$299–$350

    +May cost more when
    • After-hours service
    • OEM-specific component
    • Hard-start kit added
    −May cost less when
    • Standard replacement part in stock

  • Contactor Replacement

    Typical price$299–$350

    +May cost more when
    • After-hours service
    • Related wiring damage
    −May cost less when
    • Standard replacement part in stock

  • Thermostat Replacement

    Typical price$150–$300 for a standard thermostat or $250–$500 for a smart thermostat

    +May cost more when
    • No C-wire
    • Multi-stage heat pump or dual-fuel wiring
    −May cost less when
    • Customer-supplied compatible thermostat
    • Straightforward existing wiring

  • Condensate Drain Cleaning

    Typical price$150–$250+

    +May cost more when
    • Severe clog
    • PVC drain line requiring disassembly and treatment
    • Attic equipment
    −May cost less when
    • Accessible drain that clears with vacuuming or flushing

  • Condenser Coil Cleaning

    Typical price$150–$300

    Good to know: Routine coil cleaning may already be included in a full tune-up. This range applies primarily to standalone or more involved coil cleaning.

    +May cost more when
    • Heavily fouled coil
    • Bent fins requiring combing
    • Large equipment
    −May cost less when
    • Cleaning performed during a full tune-up

Motors & Controls

  • Condenser Fan Motor Replacement

    Typical price$450–$750

    +May cost more when
    • Variable-speed or OEM-only motor
    • Fan blade replacement
    • Equipment out of warranty
    −May cost less when
    • Universal replacement motor
    • Covered replacement part

  • Indoor Blower Motor Replacement

    Typical price$450–$900 for a standard motor or $900–$2,000+ for a variable-speed motor

    +May cost more when
    • Variable-speed motor or control module
    • Attic or crawlspace access
    −May cost less when
    • Standard motor
    • Accessible air handler
    • Replacement part under warranty
