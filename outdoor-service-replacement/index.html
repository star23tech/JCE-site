Understanding the system

What is an outdoor electrical service?

It connects the utility line to the electrical system in your house. Every property is set up a little differently, but most include:

  • Service conductors
  • Meter equipment
  • Outdoor service wiring
  • Utility-to-panel connections
  • Outdoor disconnects
  • Other service equipment

All of it has to carry the load your house pulls. When it is damaged you get rooms without power, flickering, and overheating, and it takes troubleshooting to find where.

Look beyond the immediate symptom

Restoring power is not always the same as fixing the service.

One house had lost a leg of power. We found heavy corrosion around the meter and trouble with the underground service wire. That job needed a full plan, not a quick fix.

Another meter box was in bad shape. We got the power back on that day, but the box still had to be replaced.

When the gear is that far gone, swapping one part or getting the lights back on does not fix it.

What does “one leg” mean? A home normally receives power through more than one energized conductor. If one is lost, some circuits may work while others have no power. Partial power loss should be inspected promptly.

Outdoor service FAQs

Answers to common service replacement questions.

How do I know whether the outdoor service needs replacement?

Look for rooms losing power, rust on the meter gear, or equipment that is falling apart. An inspection tells you whether a repair will hold.

Is outdoor service replacement the same as panel replacement?

No. The service brings power to the house. The panel splits it up inside. Some jobs touch one, some touch both. Here is more on electrical panel replacement.

Does the utility need to disconnect my power?

On many jobs the utility has to pull the power before we touch it, then put it back when we are done. It depends on the job.

Will the project require an electrical permit?

Most of this work needs a permit and an inspection. We handle both and fit them into the schedule.

Can corroded service equipment be repaired?

It depends on where the damage is and how far it has gone. A small isolated problem is repairable. Heavy corrosion is not.

What if the service wiring is underground?

Buried wire still goes bad. We check its condition along with the meter and the rest of the service before planning a replacement or larger wiring work.

Can the service be replaced before it fails completely?

Yes. Fixing it before it quits gives you time to plan the permit and the utility shutoff. Doing it during an outage is worse for everyone.
